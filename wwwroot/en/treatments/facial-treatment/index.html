05. Hydration · Texture & Scarring

Facial Treatment

The facial is a treatment that revives the skin, regardless of any particular skin concern. Skin needs care to be healthy, radiant, beautiful and vital.

Book a Consultation

What the treatment includes

The facial changes from client to client, according to the skin's condition and the result sought. It follows a skin diagnosis and the selection of a bespoke working protocol and the technology best suited to your skin.

  • A comprehensive skin diagnosis
  • Preparing the skin to receive active ingredients
  • A peel matched to your skin type and removal of dead cells
  • Professional massage that stimulates circulation
  • Deep cleansing of oils and extraction of comedones where needed
  • A mask matched to your skin type
  • Infusion of active ingredients deep into the cell

The electroporation device

The classic facial is paired with an electroporation device – a technology that delivers hydration deep into the cell. Actives are infused physically to cellular depth, raising the skin's hydration to a high level and lending it vitality and glow.

Who is it for?

Everyone – children, adults, women and men. Beyond being an indulgent experience, the facial contributes significantly to the skin's appearance, using the finest ingredients available in professional skincare.

Duration and frequency

The treatment lasts one hour. Recommended every 1-3 months.

Pricing & Cadence

Single treatment – 520 ILS. Series of 6 – 2,900 ILS. Series of 12 – 5,600 ILS. Recommended every 1-3 months; the treatment lasts one hour.
