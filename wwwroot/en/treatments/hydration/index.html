09. Hydration

Hydration Treatments

Hydration treatment forms part of the facial: when the skin is dehydrated, we use nourishing actives that saturate it with long-lasting moisture, delivered by an electroporation device.

Book a Consultation

How it works

Using a gentle electrical micro-current, we open temporary channels in the skin from the epidermis down to the dermis, so that any hydrophilic substance applied can permeate to the deepest cell layers. The result: skin saturated with moisture that lasts well beyond the treatment itself.

What the treatment achieves

  • Deeply moisture-saturated skin
  • Significant improvement in pigmentation
  • Improvement in wrinkles and overall skin appearance
  • Significant improvement in blemishes
