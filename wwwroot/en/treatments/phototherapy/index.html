12. Acne

Phototherapy

Phototherapy treats acne and restores the skin using light at different wavelengths – a method also practised in hospitals and in dermatology care at major clinics around the world.

Book a Consultation

What is phototherapy?

Phototherapy is a non-invasive treatment method that works by exposing the skin to different light sources, creating a photochemical response.

The method is based on blue light at specific wavelengths. The light targets the acne-causing bacterium (P. acnes) and reduces inflammatory lesions in the skin. It also suits those who, for various reasons, cannot take systemic medication.

How is the treatment performed?

Treatment is delivered with a dedicated device, either within a facial or as a series of standalone sessions.
