01. Aging

Anti-Aging

An advanced treatment method for maximal results, combining effective active ingredients with a wide range of dedicated devices and protocols – matched to your skin type and desired outcome.

Book a Consultation

What is anti-aging treatment?

Pro-aging and anti-aging treatments can begin at a young age to prevent wrinkles from forming. The treatment addresses the overall appearance of the skin, restores glow and vitality, softens existing lines and helps prevent future wrinkles and skin laxity. It works beautifully on firming, restoring volume, defining the jawline and neck, boosting collagen production and improving elastin.

The device chosen for each treatment is decided after a diagnosis, to achieve the best possible result for your skin. Over the years, Inbar developed a signature method called H.D.R, which works on multiple skin pathologies and resolves several skin concerns at once.

What the treatment gives you

  • Restored facial volume
  • Brightening of pigmentation spots
  • A clean, even skin tone
  • Radiant skin
  • Accelerated collagen and elastin production

Radiofrequency (RF) treatment

The RF treatment is designed to deliver radiance, firmness and lift. It is performed with a radiofrequency device – a pleasant treatment with no downtime and no injury. You feel a gentle warmth spreading across the face; that warmth prompts the cells to produce collagen, the protein we gradually lose as we age. With consistent sessions you notice smoother fine lines, a visibly lifted look and skin that becomes taut and luminous.

Who is it for?

For anyone who wants to see real improvement in their skin without injections – especially those troubled by wrinkles, acne scarring, lax skin or simply discomfort with how their skin looks.

The treatment experience – what to expect

On arrival you complete a skin-diagnosis form, and once suitability is confirmed the treatment begins: an initial cleanse, several peels matched to your skin type, extraction of comedones, treatment with the RF device, followed by infusion of peptides and skin-essential actives.

The treatment is pleasant, felt as comfortable warmth. Mild redness may appear afterwards and subsides during the day. Results are visible from the very first session, with the final result building over a course of treatments.

Pricing & Cadence

Treatment cost ranges from 550-1,300 ILS per session depending on the chosen technology. Recommended monthly, usually as a treatment series.
