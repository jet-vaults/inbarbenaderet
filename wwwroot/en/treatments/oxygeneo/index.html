13. Aging · Hydration

OxyGeneo

A treatment that delivers oxygen to the cells via a dedicated capsule, pairing exfoliation with heightened cellular oxygenation – for more radiant, healthy, beautiful skin.

Book a Consultation

How it works

The treatment uses a capsule containing sodium bicarbonate, magnesium and vitamin C. With a dedicated handpiece we perform gentle exfoliation; the device's action heightens oxygen at the cellular level – the body recognises the stimulus and generates internal oxygenation that is carried to the cells.

The results

  • Improvement in fine lines
  • Increased collagen production
  • Exfoliation and stimulation of new cell formation
  • More radiant, healthy, beautiful skin
