RF Treatment

A breakthrough in aesthetics: the RF device creates change deep within the cell, delivering firming, tightening and renewal – without injections or surgery.

Book a Consultation

Everything you wanted to know about facial radiofrequency

The RF device is built on radiofrequency technology – waves of electrical energy at varying frequencies and wavelengths that generate warmth through the resistance of the skin tissue. Because the tissue response is unaffected by pigment, the effect reaches considerably deeper. The warmth in the skin's deepest layers reaches the collagen and elastin fibres, creating a microscopic injury – and new fibre production begins at once. The result is a lifting of the treated area.

Since the injuries are microscopic, the skin replenishes its elastin and collagen quickly, and a meaningful difference is visible within only a few sessions – without the painful recovery of a surgical facelift.

Most modern devices are energy-based – controlled disruption of the skin so it renews itself, drawing on the skin's own natural defence mechanisms. The devices reach several depth levels, regulated by the practitioner, and prompt the elastin and collagen – responsible for the skin's suppleness and smoothness – to heal the microscopic injury. As the skin heals, it rebuilds its protein reserves, and the result is visibly tauter, younger-looking skin.

Who is it for?

It is recommended to begin treatments in the late thirties to preserve the skin's suppleness and vitality – and one can equally arrive in their seventies for a deep process of renewal, improvement and preservation.

How is the treatment performed?

The device warms the tissue to 42 degrees Celsius. As water molecules evaporate, controlled cellular disruption occurs, and renewal and collagen building begin immediately deep in the dermis. The treatment is very pleasant, with no side effects (possible mild redness that fades very quickly).

Is everyone suitable?

At your appointment, a professional diagnosis by the clinic team determines the precise details and whether the treatment is right for you.

Frequently Asked

When should I start RF treatments?
From the late thirties, to preserve the skin's suppleness and vitality – and it remains worthwhile into one's seventies for deep renewal and preservation.
Are there side effects?
The treatment is very pleasant and free of side effects – possibly mild redness that fades very quickly.
