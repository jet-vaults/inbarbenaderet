06. Hair Removal

Hair Removal

Hair removal is performed with the most advanced device on the market. Its cooled applicator makes the process pleasant and pain-free, with impressive results from the very first treatment.

Book a Consultation

What is DPC hair removal?

For anyone weary of the pain of waxing, the stubble of shaving and the mess of creams, medical aesthetics offers several options: laser, infrared and epilation – techniques that work by permanently disabling the hair root. No method can promise one-hundred-percent removal, and hormonal changes can reawaken dormant roots – but these techniques achieve the best results available.

The Inbar clinic uses a device based on DPC technology, incorporating IPL.

How it works

A beam of light is absorbed by the pigment in the hair follicle, inhibiting regrowth. The wavelength is set according to the pigment and the hair colour relative to the skin tone.

Who is it for?

Laser treatment suits larger areas. The ideal candidates have thick, dark hair, no hormonal conditions or illnesses that impair the skin's healing (vitiligo, diabetes, etc.), and are not planning pregnancy or breastfeeding. The device does not work on white hair.

The treatment plan

Treatment is structured as a series of sessions, continued until the desired results are achieved.

Frequently Asked

Is removal 100% permanent?
No method can promise one-hundred-percent removal, and hormonal changes can reawaken dormant hair roots – but permanent-root-disabling techniques achieve the best results available.
Does it hurt?
The applicator is cooled, making the process pleasant and pain-free.
Does the device work on all hair types?
It does not work on white hair. Ideal candidates have thick, dark hair.
