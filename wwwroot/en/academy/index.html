Professional Microneedling Boost Day

Professionalism, confidence – and earning potential of around 20,000 ILS a month. Four to five hours that can turn a single treatment into a growth engine for your clinic.

Sound familiar?

  • Finished a course but still don't feel confident?
  • Want to grow revenue with minimal expense?
  • Own a device but not working at the depths you should?
  • Selling single treatments instead of series?
  • Considering a device and unsure which delivers results across many cases?

That is exactly the gap we close on this day.

The theory – two hours of real professional order

  • The precise difference between microneedling and mesotherapy
  • The remodelling mechanism – what truly happens in the skin
  • A smart refresher on skin structure
  • Myths versus professional reality
  • Contraindications – who may be treated and who may not
  • Matching depths to pathology
  • Correct working techniques for every case

We learn to work on: post-acne, pitting and scars, early fine lines, skin texture, pigmentation, and thin or sensitive skin.

The business side – expertise without closing doesn't create profit

  • How to sell a series rather than a single treatment
  • How to run a phone call that leads to the clinic
  • How to explain a process convincingly without pressure
  • How to build rapport and confidence
  • How to build a clear, repeatable working protocol
  • A WhatsApp group for questions and answers

You leave with a working model – not just theoretical knowledge.

The hands-on part

  • A full demonstration on a client
  • Real-time diagnosis
  • Depth and technique selection before your eyes
  • Building an orderly protocol
  • Open questions

Who is it for?

  • Cosmeticians at the start of their journey
  • Anyone wanting to add a profitable, high-potential service
  • Those seeking minimal investment against maximal potential
  • Anyone wanting confidence with depths and with closing series

The training investment – what's included

Includes a professional Mesopen device to work with. Small groups, personal attention, close mentorship.

  • Full theoretical and practical training
  • A professional Mesopen microneedling device
  • Training booklet and protocols
  • WhatsApp group mentorship
  • Certificate of participation

Graduates may optionally purchase a professional device at a special participants-only price. Purchase is not obligatory, but lets you leave with a working tool in hand: a device suited to beginners, full instruction, post-purchase support, and a safe start with no trial and error.
