INBAR Clinic · Rishon LeZion

A clinic built around one thing: your skin

More than twenty years devoted to advanced facial treatments — no shortcuts, no passing trends. Precise diagnosis, a personal plan, and results you see in the mirror.

The Beginning

Beauty, always

My name is Inbar Ben Aderet. Born in 1976 into a family of six sisters, mother of three amazing children, happily married to Meir. As a child I was restless and hyperactive with undiagnosed attention difficulties — no one around me truly believed I would succeed. But beauty was always my calling.

At seventeen I studied professional makeup. In 2000 I completed my cosmetics training, and in 2010 — medical aesthetics. After my first daughter was born, I understood that this was the only thing I wanted to do.

The Niche

Only the face. Truly only the face.

I studied every profession within cosmetics, and understood that to be excellent at what I do — I had to choose a niche. I chose advanced facial treatment. I managed a large spa in Rishon LeZion for eight years, then opened a clinic that quickly became one of the most valued in the area.

It mattered to me to research skin in depth. No permanent makeup, no brows, no manicure — facial treatment only. I studied every skin concern: acne, pigmentation, scarring, seborrhea, psoriasis, atopic dermatitis, urticaria and more.

To be excellent at what you do — you must choose a niche. I chose facial skin.
Out of Need

Learning to ease, even when it's hard

Over the years my parents and sister fell ill with cancer and underwent chemotherapy. Out of their need, I trained in oncology aesthetics — I wanted to know how to ease the skin even when the body struggles.

When my father passed away, I suffered from atopic dermatitis myself. Every doctor offered a steroid cream; none solved the problem at its root. I went to study cosmetic acupuncture — which cleared my atopic skin completely.

Body & Mind

One equation

From the need to support clients who open their hearts to me, I trained as an NLP coach, giving my clients powerful tools for the road ahead. I deeply believe body and mind are one equation — when we feel good within, it shows on our skin.

And of course, I cannot rest. I went on to study chemistry, with a dream of developing a skincare brand that creates real change — true intra-cellular work that lets every client look in the mirror and enjoy healthy, radiant skin.

Body and mind are one equation. When we feel good within — it shows on the skin.
Today

A clinic, a team, an academy

Today I run a clinic in Rishon LeZion with an amazing team, and teach courses that train cosmeticians to be the next messengers of this profession.
