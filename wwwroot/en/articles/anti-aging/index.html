Anti-Aging Treatment with RF Radio-Frequency Technology

Over 40 and ready to see real improvement in your skin — without injections? The RF anti-aging treatment delivers firmness, lift, and radiance, with no downtime and no injury to the skin.

Are you 40 or over, ready to finally see your skin change for the better — without injections? Struggling with wrinkles, acne scarring, loss of firmness, and discomfort with the way you look? This treatment was made for you.

What Does the Anti-Aging Treatment Give You?

  • Restored facial volume
  • Brightening of pigmentation and dark spots
  • A clean, even skin tone
  • A radiant complexion
  • Accelerated collagen and elastin production

The Radio-Frequency Treatment

The goal of the treatment is to give the skin radiance, firmness, and lift. It is performed with an RF-technology device — very comfortable, with no downtime and no injury to the skin. You feel a gentle warmth spreading across the face; that warmth prompts the cells to produce collagen, the protein we lose as we age. With consistent sessions, you notice a change in fine lines, in the skin's overall appearance, and in its lift — the skin becomes luminous and taut.

How a Session Unfolds

On arrival you complete a skin assessment form, and if the treatment suits you, we begin. The session includes an initial cleanse, a sequence of peels matched to your skin type, extraction of comedones, and the radio-frequency treatment itself — followed by the infusion of peptides and actives essential to the skin.

The treatment is pleasant, accompanied by a comfortable sensation of warmth. Mild redness may appear afterwards and fades within the day. Results are visible from the first session, though the full effect settles in over a series of treatments.

A Pleasure to Meet You — I'm Inbar Ben Aderet

Married, and a mother to three sweet children. Let me take you back 25 years, to the time when I myself suffered from acne and facial scarring. Every blemish stayed with me for years as a mark; I went through countless cosmeticians and spent a fortune searching for a solution. I was frustrated and insecure — every time someone spoke to me, I felt they were examining only the flaws in my skin. It ran my life.

I remember wanting to study cosmetics, and my aunt telling me: "You — with a face like yours — who would ever come to you?" I was so hurt by that sentence that in that very moment I made a decision: no one would define my success, and no one would tell me whether I deserved it.

And from there — 25 years forward: a wonderful clinic that has already helped over 10,000 clients let go of their blemishes and their scars. I am moved every single time I see you leave my clinic full of confidence, feeling worthy. Each of you is a diamond — I just do a little polishing, so your light can shine through.
