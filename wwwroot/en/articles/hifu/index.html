HIFU

HIFU is a non-invasive treatment based on focused ultrasound waves that reach the skin's deepest layers, refining its texture and creating a cumulative lifting effect.

HIFU is a remarkably effective treatment designed to heal and restore the skin using ultrasound technology that refines skin texture and creates a lifting effect. It reaches the very deepest layers of the skin — a non-invasive device built on powerful, focused sound-wave technology.

How It Works

HIFU energy passes through the skin in a precisely focused beam, leaving the surface untouched, and allows the focal point to reach the uppermost layer of the SMAS (the superficial musculo-aponeurotic system). There, the ultrasound acts on the collagen and elastin fibres: the fibres contract, the volume of the SMAS decreases, and an immediate lifting effect is created. The technology also stimulates the production of new collagen and elastin fibres.

When Do Results Appear?

Improvement becomes visible around two months after the first session. Because the treatment creates a cumulative lifting effect, significant changes continue to appear over the following months: collagen keeps renewing and firming the facial skin, the complexion improves, and the results are maintained over time.

Who Is It For?

The treatment is suited to lifting areas where the skin has begun to sag — the jowls along the jawline, the neck, and the face as a whole.

The treatment is not suitable for those who have experienced facial nerve paralysis (facial palsy), heart conditions, cancer, or diabetes.
